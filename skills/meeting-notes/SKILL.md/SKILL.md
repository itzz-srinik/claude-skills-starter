# Meeting Notes

Produce this structure:

## Summary
Two to three sentences on what the meeting was about.

## Decisions
A bullet list of what was agreed.

## Action items
A table with columns: Task, Owner, Due date. Write "Unassigned" or "No date" instead of guessing.

## Open questions
Anything raised but not resolved.

Do not invent details that are not in the notes.
